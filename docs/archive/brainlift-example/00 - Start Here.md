## What a BrainLift is (in 30 seconds)

A **BrainLift** is a structured document that captures *your* expert thinking on one well-bounded topic — the facts you've gathered, the concepts underneath them, the decision rules you've derived, and the contrarian positions you're willing to defend in public. It's built to be read by both humans and AI, so anyone (or any model) working on that topic can make a sound decision without re-reading the whole literature.

It is not a summary, a wiki page, or a report. The defining move is that **you take positions you could be proven wrong about** — and you show the evidence trail that got you there.

## The one rule that matters most

**BrainLifts are not AI-generated.** You may use AI to gather raw facts and find experts. You must **write the thinking yourself** — the summaries, the insights, and especially the spiky points of view. That knowledge only becomes *yours* by passing through your own head.

> A long, polished BrainLift that appeared in a single sitting is the exact red flag this rule exists to catch. The value is in the reaction, not the construction.

This is non-negotiable and it shapes everything else in this kit. Details in `06 - Working With AI`.

## How to use this kit

1. Read **01** and **02** to understand what a BrainLift is and how it's built.
2. Open **03** and **04** side by side — read the annotated walkthrough while looking at the real thing. This is where it clicks.
3. Read **05** and start your **first daily note today**. You do *not* start by writing a full BrainLift; you start with one short note a day and let it compound.
4. Keep **07** (templates) and **08** (checklist) open while you work.

The goal isn't to produce a document. It's to become the person in the room who has actually thought a topic through — and can prove it.
