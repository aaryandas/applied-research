## Cross-referencing convention

As you write, tag claims with the tiers they rest on so the document stays traceable:

- In an SPOV: `(KT2, KT4)` or `(Insight 3)`
- In an Insight: `[Reading 7]` or `(KT1)`
- In a Blueprint step: `(Insight 2, KT6)`

The test: a skeptical reader can follow any claim down to a citation in under a minute.
