# Canvas

- **Job and outcome.** Operate, with reading inside the selected branch. A builder learning attention for a coding-agent retrieval layer arrives from the question door, a source, or Back. They should locate the next lesson, trace a cited answer, and see how their own insight and thesis depend on evidence without leaving the project. Proof: find Next in overview, inspect both source chips, return to the same viewport, and connect two cards from different lessons by writing above them.
- **Direction.** One infinite Canvas. A fixed Learning Path holds a root, four topics and twelve lessons with mixed word statuses. Question and answer cards branch beneath two lessons; notes terminate branches. An insight sits above its two supporting trees; a thesis sits above the insight with a claim and “what would prove me wrong.” Vertical position and downward connectors carry elevation; words and material reinforce it. Inherit the original material system in `docs/designs/Design System Canvas Setup/Applied Research - Design System.dc.html`: dark ground, Newsreader human text and questions, Fraunces Brief, Familjen Grotesk interface and AI text, warm human marks, cold interactions, Phosphor Light chrome. No lamp or invented mastery score.
- **Interaction and layout.** Brief: “I'm building a retrieval layer for a coding agent and need to understand why attention scales the way it does”. A single scrolling right sidebar follows the selection, showing lessons, statuses, sources and questions. The bottom field says “What do you want to understand?” with “Find new sources” off initially. Drag empty ground to pan; wheel/trackpad pans, pinch or modifier-wheel zooms around the pointer; controls fit or focus. Select two cards, then Connect: an empty human writing field appears above them with their evidence in the sidebar. A visible card handle drags a connector out to an attached question draft. Citation chips open a small Reader demonstration at the cited location; Back restores viewport and selection. Human writing is never generated.
- **Scope and ranges.** One throwaway HTML file, inline CSS/JS, no framework, desktop target 1440×900 and larger. Three comparisons: Compact uses short previews and curved branches; Expanded gives full answers more room; Right angles uses aligned connectors for tracing crowded branches. Ten initial event cards, plus a 30-event-card stress view; path nodes are counted separately. Overview retains card types, titles, lesson status and elevation; close view reveals full text and citation chips. Core examples include two note leaves, two cross-lesson evidence branches, one insight and one thesis. Fonts and icons are embedded. Source text is a prototype fixture; the paper explanation is checked against Vaswani §3.2, while the supplied lecture locator is illustrative.
- **States, boundaries and decisions.** This is an interaction study, not source ingestion or live AI. Drafts can be saved or cancelled; unsupported answers are visibly unsupported, and loading/failed/partial states can be inspected without deleting the question. Empty questions leave the Learning Path intact. Sidebar and inputs remain usable at reduced window sizes; focus, Escape and reduced motion are supported. Decisions still belong to Aaryan: density at 30 cards, connector shape, whether elevation reads, citation-chip appearance, and the drag-from-card gesture.
