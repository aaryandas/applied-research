# Applied Research: ubiquitous language

Plain terms only. This file is a glossary, not a spec. Decisions live in `.scratch/surfaces/` and, once assembled, `docs/gauntlet/SURFACES.md`.

## Things

- **Project**: one Brief, one Learning Path, one Canvas, one Playbook, its sources.
- **Brief**: one human-owned sentence, "I'm building X and need to understand Y". The AI may draft it; the human owns it after any edit.
- **Source**: a paper, a chapter of a textbook you own, a lecture (with its transcript and video), or an HTML/EPUB document, ingested into sentences. Immutable once ingested.
- **Sentence**: the anchor. Every cited claim points at sentence ids. A lecture's sentence is a timestamped transcript segment.
- **Learning Path**: the tree the app generates: subject at the root, topics as branches, lessons as leaves; the fixed skeleton of the Canvas.
- **Topic**: a branch of the Learning Path; can be hidden.
- **Lesson**: one concept, a leaf of the Learning Path, with the sources it draws from and a status.
- **Status**: Not started, In progress, Completed, Mastered. Words, never a number. Mastered only from a passed check.
- **Edge**: the first lesson with an unmet prerequisite. **Next**: the shallowest lesson not Completed whose prerequisites are Completed or Mastered.
- **Canvas**: the one view where the Learning Path, your questions, and your thinking live; has elevation (ground, insights, theses).
- **Card**: a view of exactly one event on the Canvas.
- **Question**: human, asked from a selection, the Canvas text box, dragging from a card, or the pointing assistant.
- **Answer**: AI, always beneath its question, always cited.
- **Explainer**: an AI card that shows one concept in seconds: a clip (Manim) or an interactive scene (React Three Fiber), citing the sentences it explains.
- **Note**: human words anchored to a passage or a card. Level 2.
- **Insight**: human words standing on one or more cards or passages; created by Connect on the Canvas or the Insight verb in the Reader. Level 3.
- **Thesis**: a human position standing on insights: claim, evidence, what would prove me wrong; kind Critique or Gap. Level 4. Created by Take a position.
- **Stress-test**: the AI's strongest cited case against a thesis, on request.
- **Check**: a short written answer graded by the model against a lesson's sources, with cited reasoning; disputable. **Place me** runs checks adaptively across the tree.
- **Playbook**: the read-only compiled view of theses, insights, and cited facts, for reading back and export.
- **Context pack**: the Playbook exported as files coding agents read, plus the local MCP server's read-only tools.
- **Unsupported**: a claim whose citation did not verify; a badge, never prose.

## Rules of the language

- The AI writes only cited facts and explanations (levels 1). Notes, insights, and theses are human-only; no record at level 3 or 4 can have an AI author.
- The AI never speaks first. Unsolicited offers are disabled; reopening an explanation already requested is not an offer.
- Nothing is shown without a sentence behind it except human words. Web search finds sources and is never cited.
