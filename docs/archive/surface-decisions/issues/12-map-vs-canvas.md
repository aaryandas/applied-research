# 12 · Map and Canvas: one surface or two
Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 05

## Question
Are the Map (the domain by prerequisite depth, computed from sources) and the Canvas (the human's branching questions) one surface or two? If two, how are they linked, and what does each show that the other must not? If one, how does a computed, fixed layout coexist with free branching?

## Inputs
Resolution of *Canvas semantics*; `docs/mvp-prd.md` S13 (fixed layout, no dragging); `docs/designs/prompt-2-screens.md` screen 3; critique §7 and §12 on the knowledge metaphor and the removed lamp.

## Resolution must state
One or two surfaces, with the job of each in a sentence. The link between them (can a canvas question be located on the map? does the map show question density?). What each must never show.

## Starting recommendation
Two. The Map is computed and fixed: sources by prerequisite depth, known and unknown territory, the curriculum. The Canvas is free: questions, answers, and the human's thoughts. Link: a canvas question is locatable on the map at the depth of the sources it cites; a map band shows how many of your questions land there. The Map never holds human writing; the Canvas never holds a source that has not been asked about.

## Answer
Decided 2026-09-06 with Aaryan, superseding an earlier same-day answer (two linked views) after Aaryan reopened it with the Wondering learning-map screenshot (`references/canvas/wondering-learning-map.png`).

**There is no Map. The Canvas is one view whose skeleton is the Learning Path.**

**The Learning Path** is a tree generated by the app and laid out automatically; nodes are not dragged. Root: the project's subject (from the Brief, or the source's title when the project starts from a source). Branches: topics. Leaves: lessons, each one concept. Every lesson has a status: Not started, In progress, Completed, Mastered (definitions in *May the AI speak first*, addendum). Selecting any node opens the right sidebar with the node's lessons and their statuses, and for each lesson the sources it draws from (a lecture with a timestamp, a chapter, a paper section); clicking a source opens the Reader at that spot.

**Your cards branch off the lesson nodes.** When you ask a question, it attaches to the lesson its cited sources belong to and appears as a card branching from that lesson node, in the grammar of the Wondering canvas: answers beneath questions, notes as leaves, explainers, insights, and theses hanging off those cards. Zoomed out you see the tree and its statuses; zoomed in on a lesson you see your question trees. Free arrangement exists only inside a lesson's area. The text box at the bottom asks about the selected lesson (or the root if none is selected).

**Before you have asked anything, the Canvas shows the Learning Path alone.** That is its empty state for questions and its full state for the path.

**What it never shows.** No source appears as a node; sources live in the side panel and as citation chips. No status is ever a number or a percentage.

### Addendum 2026-09-06 (from prototype 17)
"Nodes are not dragged" is withdrawn: Learning Path nodes are movable like any card; the tree's structure (which lesson belongs to which topic) stays as generated unless a topic is hidden or the path is extended.
