What makes
attention work?

A reading study of scaled dot-product attention.

Attention connects a query to the keys that matter. The dot products become weights through a softmax, and those weights determine how the values are combined.

The detail worth pausing on is the scale: as the key dimension grows, the dot products can grow too. Dividing by the square root of that dimension keeps the inputs to the softmax from becoming too large.

Attention(Q, K, V) = softmaxQKT√dkV

Study paraphrase · §3.2.1, not a verbatim paper excerpt

AI explanation

Why divide by √dk?

Under the paper’s simplifying assumptions, the dot product has variance dk. Scaling by √dk brings that variance back to 1, helping avoid an overly concentrated softmax.
