# Grand landscape opening-screen study

Status: approved by the user on September 5, 2026 ("Love this let's go with this"). This is the selected desktop opening-screen artwork and composition, not a production app implementation. Field Atlas remains the provisional broader interface system.

## Direction

A complete continuous landscape replaces the rejected floating island. A close viewpoint beneath a monumental apple tree provides scale; its canopy is lightly cropped overhead and its trunk anchors the right. A meadow flows into a deep mountain valley with a winding river, distant peaks and luminous clouds. One detached crimson apple and fine upward trails express downward motion. No figure. The exact center remains reserved for the app opening prompt.

## Interface

The preview imports the actual shared fonts.css, tokens.css and styles.css. It uses Source Serif 4, Hanken Grotesk and the existing button/button-cream classes. A 1440 × 900 desktop canvas scales as a whole for the study viewer. The artwork is separate from the live HTML text. The interface toggle and preview dialogs work; project creation is not connected. This is a desktop composition study, not a mobile adaptation.

## Initial prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Create one original, exquisitely art-directed 16:10 landscape illustration for a desktop research app. The attached World Labs screenshot is ONLY a reference for its luminous sculptural landscape, fine engraved surface detail, blue/ivory color relationship and extraordinary sense of place. Do not reproduce its typography, layout, glass sphere or rocks. No text or interface in the generated image.

The scene: A GRAND, COMPLETE, continuous earthly landscape viewed from beneath the outer canopy of a monumental living apple tree. The foreground meadow extends across the ENTIRE bottom edge and flows into a vast deep mountain valley, with receding emerald slopes, winding river, distant blue alpine peaks and luminous ivory cumulus clouds. It must feel like a place one can walk into, vast and breathtaking, not a little object against blank space. No island, no floating land, no isolated diorama, no globe, no arch.

Composition: close low viewpoint. The majestic thick graceful ivory-brown tree trunk anchors the right third, rooted naturally in the hillside. Large boughs extend across the upper frame, lush jade/emerald apple foliage lightly cropped along the top; tree fills most of the image height, with clear branching and airy foliage clusters. Full landscape fills the lower 35–40%, with layers of distant terrain giving immense depth. Beautiful clouds populate the upper-left distant sky and far right, luminous and dimensional, leaving a naturally calm blue opening at the exact center. Keep x=34–66%, y=40–59% mostly even medium-deep blue sky around #285f8c so small warm-white opening-prompt text can later be placed there in code. This is a subtle opening within a FULL scene, not an enormous empty field or graphic cutout. Mountain skyline stays mostly below y=62%. The tree canopy frames rather than obscures this center.

One crisp crimson apple is visibly falling at x=27%, y=48%, clear of foliage and terrain, its width about 2.5% of frame. It is suspended well below an apple-bearing branch, detached. Exactly three short, elegant, tapered ivory motion strokes trail upward above the apple to communicate downward movement. Restrained, fine lines, no glowing magical effect. A few tiny muted apples may be visible attached within the foliage, but this falling apple is the vivid focal accent. No people.

Art style: match the reference's bright sculptural rendering, luminous cream highlights, cool blue shadows, beautiful finely incised contours selectively tracing natural forms. Refined contemporary dreamlike landscape with the craft of hand-colored intaglio, convincing space and modeled volume. Fresh greens, mineral blue, butter-ivory sunlight, red apple. Clean blue sky, fine detail on tree and terrain rather than noise everywhere. Not a dark antique engraving, not sepia, not a flat vector cartoon, not a photograph, not generic glossy 3D fantasy. Quiet awe and possibility, not dramatic apocalypse. Full-bleed continuous landscape without frame.

## Color refinement prompt

Refine the attached landscape illustration with ONE targeted change: change the vegetation palette from yellow-olive to fresh, vibrant natural jade and emerald greens. Preserve exactly the composition, tree silhouette, full valley landscape, sky, luminous ivory clouds, mountains, falling red apple and fine upward motion trails. Preserve the delicate engraved art style. The leaves should have living green midtones and cool deep green shadows, with restrained creamy sunlight on selected edges, not yellow all over. Likewise make the meadow and valley slopes richer green rather than ochre, retaining their depth and subtle blue atmospheric recession. Keep all blue sky and ivory highlights as they are. Do not add text, people, objects, shapes, borders, or artificial glow. Keep the same wide 16:10 crop.
