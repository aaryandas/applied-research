# Field Atlas — direction contract

The user asks for a new Applied Research design system addressing the original teardown, with the blue, engraved, surreal landscape atmosphere of their World Labs reference. This replaces the earlier visual restrictions, not the product’s human-authorship doctrine.

The unique mechanism is reading a paper, forming a reaction, and carrying that reaction into a human-owned Playbook with its evidence intact. The audience is a builder entering an unfamiliar field, alternating between exploration and concentration. Scientific illustration and editorial publishing supply the cultural world; working tools supply its discipline.

The concept is an illustrated field atlas. Mineral blue and an original engraved arch establish a threshold into discovery. Warm opaque surfaces protect reading. Source Serif 4 provides expressive scale and sustained reading; Hanken Grotesk handles tools. The arch is an original asset, not World Labs’ globe, mountains, or branding.

The first viewport should feel expansive and authored, with live serif typography on the image’s negative space and one direct route into the workspace. The path is arrival → working Reader → your reaction → Workbench → Playbook → system foundations → craft. The signature interaction is keeping a thought without losing its source or authorship, not moving the landscape.

The direction extends to dense reading, human composition, export, source inspection, preferences, day/evening environments, empty and error states. Decorative imagery never sits behind sustained reading or writing. No ambient movement; 150 ms action feedback, 220 ms settle/reveal, 700 ms initial arrival, immediate equivalents with reduced motion.

Seven grounded candidates considered: scientific journal, annotated laboratory notebook, illustrated field atlas, observatory instrument panel, literary review, proofing desk, transit diagram. The habitual category dashboard and the opposite immersive 3D canvas were rejected as poor matches for the task. Impeccable seed ad8ccae5 assigned index 3 and was retrieved successfully after a restricted-network retry. The explicit user reference independently pins the field-atlas direction and outranks random catalog alternatives. Industrial hazard labels, gravity gardens, and console grammar would displace that specific vision; their relevant discipline is literal state labeling, coherent spatial behavior, and immediate controls, carried here without their motifs. No alternative visual board or generated UI comp was approved; the supplied screenshot is inspiration, not a pixel-reproduction contract.

Success means a recognizable, illustrated identity plus a usable source → reaction → synthesis reference. It does not mean a production Electron renderer, live LLM integration, ingestion, a secure vault, or a working multi-file Context pack. Those remain explicitly identified handoff work.
