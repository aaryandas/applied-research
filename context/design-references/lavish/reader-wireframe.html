The five commitments

1 · Paper stays center-stage ③

Highlight a passage → ask → the answer renders under the highlight, with a section citation. No chat panel, no new window. This is the direct attack on the mid-read rabbit hole (premise 2 — where your 10 hrs/week die).

kills the context break

2 · Argument map = sidebar list, not canvas ④

Claims with evidence meters, unstated warrants exposed, cross-source rebuttals ("Lost in the Middle" rebuts C2). Argument mapping failed for 200 years on construction cost + diagram-switching cost — a compact list anchored beside the text avoids both (premise 5).

the anti-Kialo move

3 · Provenance tags everywhere

Every element is labeled AI or YOU. The brainlift doctrine as architecture, not guideline: AI constructs DOK1 scaffolding; DOK3/DOK4 fields literally cannot be AI-authored (premise 3).

doctrine in the schema

4 · Dispute → POV draft ④

Reacting to a claim (agree / dispute / unsure) is the capture mechanism. A dispute immediately offers a spiky-POV draft box — the "reaction engine" moment no competitor has. Reactions accumulate into the brainlift instead of being an end-of-session chore.

the wedge interaction

5 · Export Context Pack as a first-class object ① ⑤

The rail ends in brainlift.md + agent-context.md — only human-authored POVs included. Research ends in an artifact your coding agent builds from (premise 4, the unclaimed territory). Source discovery also lives in the rail ("+ Find sources…") so search never opens a new tab.
