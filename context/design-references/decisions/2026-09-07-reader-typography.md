# Reader typography and surface correction

The founder supplied [this Reader screenshot](2026-09-07-reader-visual-reference.png) and wrote: “I think this version looks better just because of the darker background color and better spacing/sizing of text.”

This pins the continuous dark Reader ground and the smaller, tighter typography. The original Claude type artboard remains the family/scale authority: Newsreader 22px/1.3 weight 500 for Reader headings and 18px/1.55 for reading/human writing; Familjen Grotesk 13–14px/1.5 for UI and AI; Martian Mono 12px for navigation/coordinates; Fraunces for project titles and briefs with optical sizing and WONK enabled.

Round 2 now uses locally served variable Newsreader, Fraunces and Martian Mono files, with provenance in the prototype assets. Reader and its rail share #0c0f12; panel separation uses #22272c. The rail is flat, with a single divider, while controls retain the previously selected rounded Field Atlas treatment. Expanded explanation padding is reduced. The screenshot has a display color profile, so raw screenshot RGB values are not treated as CSS tokens.

The screenshot preference does not reinstate the rejected Workbench, Reader spine or depth path bar. The Insights/Sources flow and rounded controls remain under review. This is a prototype revision, not final approval of the full desktop product or a renderer implementation. Two observed human insights were preserved in the prototype before refreshing.
