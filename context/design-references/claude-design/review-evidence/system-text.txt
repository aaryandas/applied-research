01 · Principles and posture
One page · read before anything else
An instrument, not a coach, in a reading room.
Applied Research finds papers, maps a domain by prerequisite depth, and puts the paper on the whole screen. The AI builds scaffolding — sources, explanations, argument maps. The interface exists to provoke and capture the user's reactions. The AI may author facts. Only the human authors insights and theses, and the design must show that without a single label.
Never speaks first
No nudges. No "you haven't reacted in a while." One exemption: acknowledgement. Save settles (220ms) and offers Undo for 5s as a text button; no toast.
Renders state, continuously
How much you've reacted to, how deep you are, what you know versus don't. The seeing is the provocation.
The paper is the screen
When reading, chrome recedes. Column max 68ch, centered.
Affordance at point of contact
A text selection, a dark region on the map — and nowhere else. Shortcut hints appear inside the toolbar on hover ("Explain ⌘K"), not only in tooltips.

3.2 Attention
An attention function can be described as mapping a query and a set of key-value pairs to an output, where the query, keys, values, and output are all vectors. The output is computed as a weighted sum of the values, where the weight assigned to each value is computed by a compatibility function of the query with the corresponding key.

06 · Motion · five signatures, three durations
Nothing here moves. Nothing in the product moves on its own.
Cause
Motion is a consequence of something the user did. No loops, no ambient, no attention-seeking.
Curve
Reveals, not bounces. cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1), interruptible. Three durations only: 150 touch (hover fill, toolbar, tab underline, citation lift) · 220 reveal (card open, layer push, peek, citation landing) · 900 reward (wash spread, Playbook compile).
Eye line
The paper never reflows above the reader's eye. Cards move only what is below; layers land on the exact line.
Reduced motion
Cuts all five to the end state. Not shorter durations — no motion.

Brief
The project's opening sentence: "I'm building X and need to understand Y." Fraunces, wash backdrop.

Project home. Concepts as horizontal layers by prerequisite depth, foundations at the bottom.

The lit (dark) or washed (light) region · the 1px cold line where it ends. Warm = what the human made; cold = what the interface offers.

Sources
Papers only in v1.
Explain ⌘K · Note · Counter · Idea
The four toolbar actions; the Explain submit is “Explain”. Card actions: Ask follow-up · Note in my words. There is no Highlight.
I was missing something · The paper has a problem · Still open
Counter resolutions. The second spawns a Critique.
Rabbit hole · "3 deep"
A pushed layer and its depth.
Path bar · Edge strip
Breadcrumb atop a layer with the depth count · one dot per layer on the reader's edge, current dot is the mark, click to pop.
Spine · "5 asked · 0 said"
A 1px line the length of the paper; left ticks = questions the AI answered, right ticks = things the human said. Sidebar row, reader edge, Workbench.
Citation
§3.2.1 · Vaswani 2017 · §3.2.1. Mono lowercase 12px ink-2, unboxed, sentence end. Hover goes cold and peeks, click jumps. Boxed only as an evidence object. No pages, no arXiv ids, no circled numerals.
Type label · Margin marker
note · counter · idea · question, mono, uncolored, stateless · the pen mark in the gutter beside a reacted paragraph; only counter has open/resolved. Hit 24px.
Arguments · Questions · Annotations
Right sidebar tabs. Always about the thing in front of you.
Workbench: Reactions · Insights · Theses · Playbook
Tabs are the ladder, each with its count. Every rung shows the rung below on the left and a place to write on the right. "An insight from these" · "Save insight".

A defended, falsifiable claim, in two kinds.

The AI's cited case against a thesis, from the user's own papers only.

The ladder. AI may author Facts only. Never show DOK numbers.
