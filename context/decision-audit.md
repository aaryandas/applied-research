# Technology and Architecture Decision Audit

Reviewed 2026-09-07 against the imported Applied Research knowledge base, original project documents in both migration archives, and the current repository. This is a reconstruction of recorded decisions, not a new stack selection or verification of historical package/model availability.

## Correction to the repository summary

Substantial technology and architecture work predates the scaffold. Calling storage, providers, authentication, telemetry, and sync simply “undecided” erased that history. The September 6 statement that stack and hosting were open warranted reconciliation; it did not establish npm or automatically reject each earlier choice. The blanket “historical-reference” banners were added during migration and are not evidence that each underlying decision was individually withdrawn.

The current npm setup is an agent-selected scaffold default, not a recovered founder decision. The user recalls choosing pnpm. No pnpm mention was located in the 88 project Markdown notes or the first-party text inspected in the two original-file archives. The September 5 architecture interview is explicitly referenced by the Wayfinder and surface map but its substantive record has not been recovered from those materials. Do not interpret this missing evidence as a decision against pnpm. Recover that record before declaring package-manager history settled. No package-manager migration is part of this audit.

## How to read the chronology

- September 3: researched server/web stack and founder preferences, including Railway and Gemini.
- September 4: explicit founder pivot to cross-platform Electron; the end of Presearch supersedes its earlier server/web table. Handoff and MVP PRD consolidate that desktop baseline.
- September 5–6: resolved surface decisions expand the product and explicitly change event types, cloud/tier behavior, analytics defaults, and permitted AI loops. Wayfinder says a separate September 5 grill session decided implementation details; its scope exclusion is not a statement that nothing was decided.
- September 6 onward: Learning Path primacy and external practical work are accepted; stack/hosting were reopened for reconciliation. Build Readiness and Product Decision Record contain labeled proposals, not replacements for every prior decision.
- Current scaffold: Electron/React/TypeScript plus CI is implemented. Effect was requested later and v3 source was vendored, but no Effect application runtime exists yet.

## Research findings versus accepted selections

The later parsing research recommends Datalab accurate as the hosted quality tier, Mistral as a price tier, and MinerU hybrid (or Chandra subject to license fit) for GPU self-hosting, with Docling as an MIT fallback. It retains HTML/TEI-first ingestion and application-owned offsets. This is a researched revision to the original Mistral default; the recovered material does not demonstrate founder ratification of a final replacement. [Research - Paper Parsing 2026](obsidian://open?vault=Obsidian%20Vault&file=Applied%20Research%2FSources%2FApplied%20Research%20-%20Research%20-%20Paper%20Parsing%202026)

Those findings support the sourced curriculum, but do not by themselves choose a curriculum planner, search index, or hosting vendor. [Research - Courses And Scholarly Apis](obsidian://open?vault=Obsidian%20Vault&file=Applied%20Research%2FSources%2FApplied%20Research%20-%20Research%20-%20Courses%20And%20Scholarly%20Apis)
